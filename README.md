# David & Goliath Kids Game Assets (Ages 3–6)

Bright, colorful, **scripture-faithful** character videos for a preschool David and Goliath game.  
Everything is kept in complete context of the original **Hebrew text of 1 Samuel 17** (Greek is for the New Testament). Soft, non-scary, faith-centered cartoons perfect for 3–6 year olds.

## Scriptural Accuracy (1 Samuel 17 Hebrew Bible)

- David = דָּוִד, youngest of eight sons of יִשַׁי, from בֵּית לֶחֶם יְהוּדָה. Described as אַדְמֹנִי (ruddy) with יְפֵה מַרְאֶה.
- Goliath = גָּלְיָת from גַּת, height שֵׁשׁ אַמּוֹת וָזֶרֶת, full armor details preserved in kid-friendly form.
- Setting: עֵמֶק הָאֵלָה (Valley of Elah).
- No invented plot, no modern elements, no graphic violence. Faith in YHWH is central.

## How to Use with Grok Bot Agents

1. Download the four MP4 files from the `videos/` folder.
2. Feed them to your game-building agents as the official character assets.
3. Instruct agents: “Use only these videos. Keep every visual and spoken line faithful to the Hebrew of 1 Samuel 17. Target age 3–6: simple language, bright colors, short levels, reward courage and trust in God.”

Suggested game flow:
- Meet young Dawid the shepherd (videos 1 + 3)
- Hear Golyath’s challenge (video 2)
- Sling practice mini-game
- The battle cutscene (video 4)
- Celebration & memory verse: “The battle is the LORD’s!” (1 Sam 17:47)
